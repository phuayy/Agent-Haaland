## Task: post-mortem prose

You are given the incident's event timeline as structured facts (already
rendered as a table — do not reproduce or alter it) plus the diagnosis,
remediation, and any human decisions recorded. Write the prose sections
only: summary, customer impact, root cause narrative, resolution narrative,
what went well, what didn't, and action items.

Do not restate facts you were given in structured form — narrate around
them. Do not invent timestamps, actors, or events that are not in the
timeline you were given.
